Privacy warning: This tracker is operated by the person hosting this server, not Jagex or RuneLite. Client connection is opt-in. When enabled, your RuneScape name, skill stats and quest points are sent to that operator. Additional switches share banks/group storage, position/world, live XP, your own GIM chat and gameplay alerts. The operator can read, store and share uploads; enabled chat/alerts may also be sent to Discord. Member viewing permissions do not hide data from the operator. Only connect to a host you trust. Disabling sharing or locking this page does not delete previous uploads; ask the host to remove them. Never enter your RuneScape password.

Group owner: type !verify in the bot channel to receive your private owner key by DM, then generate character-specific tokens in Manage group. Members: use the token your owner gives you here and in the RuneLite plugin's Member token setting; no Discord verification is needed. Never share the owner's key or enter a RuneScape password. Access is saved in this tab's session storage so refresh keeps you signed in. Lock clears it. Browser session restore or tab duplication may retain it.
